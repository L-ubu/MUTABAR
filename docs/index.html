02What you get

◆ 82 creatures, 6 rarities

Animals, mythological beasts, famous monsters, originals and hybrids — from Common to Mutagen, with shiny variants at +15% stats.

◆ AI-narrated combat

Free-form attacks narrated by a local LLM. Eight mutation types play rock-paper-scissors underneath the prose.

◆ Lootboxes done right

Three mutabox tiers with a CS:GO-style rolling reveal. Golden shimmer, legendary rainbow, mutagen pulse.

◆ Idle income

Assign creatures to the idle arena for passive mutagen — offline earnings calculated on next launch, up to 8 hours.

◆ Roguelite runs

Escalating waves, boss every 5th round, and random between-wave events: Healing Spring, Mysterious Trader, Shrine.

◆ Actually native

Borderless window anchored to the menu bar icon, 4 switchable themes — Tokyo Night, Phosphor, Frosted Glass, Adaptive.

04Why it exists

The decisions worth knowing about, written down.

Games should fit in the gaps

Not every game needs a fullscreen window and an hour. MUTABAR lives where your cursor already is, and a battle fits in a compile.

Local LLM, not an API bill

Combat narration runs on a local model. No keys, no latency, no cost per battle — and it works offline.

ASCII art is a feature

Every creature in the Mutadex is hand-drawn ASCII. A menu bar game should wear its terminal soul proudly.
